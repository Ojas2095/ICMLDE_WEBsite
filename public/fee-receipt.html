Download your fee receipt

Enter your Paper ID and the email you registered with. When they match a confirmed payment, your receipt appears here, ready to print or save as a PDF.

The address you used when you registered or paid.

Can’t find your receipt? Receipts appear only after the finance team confirms your payment. If you paid more than a week ago, write to info@icmlde.org with your Paper ID and UTR number.

How it works

  1. Pay the registration fee and register with your Paper ID.
  2. The finance team checks your payment against the bank statement.
  3. Once confirmed, enter your Paper ID and email here to get the receipt.
  4. Print it, or choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog to keep a copy.
