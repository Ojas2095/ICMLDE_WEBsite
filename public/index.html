About ICMLDE

The 5th International Conference on Machine Learning and Data Engineering (ICMLDE 5.0) is an international forum for presenting original research results and sharing practical development experience across engineering domains. It brings together researchers and application developers from artificial intelligence and data engineering, with a focus on algorithms and applications that address current challenges in engineering and technology.

Every session includes expert talks and presentations from young researchers. Researchers, academics and professionals worldwide are invited to present original, unpublished work: theory, practice, experiments and reviews.

All papers in the ICMLDE series are published in Procedia Computer Science (Elsevier). ICMLDE proceedings are indexed in Scopus, Web of Science and DBLP.

Tracks and themes

ICMLDE 2026 invited original, unpublished research in four theme groups. Call for papers (PDF)

  1. 1

    Machine learning foundations

    System design and optimisation, supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement and statistical learning.

    Transfer learning, extreme learning machines, kernel-based learning, Bayesian learning, instruction-based learning, adversarial machine learning

  2. 2

    Deep learning and data engineering

    Optimisation algorithms for deep neural networks, feedforward, convolutional and recurrent networks, and regularisation.

    Sequence modelling, deep generative models, GANs, multi-layered network inference, tensors for deep learning, multi-scale architectures

  3. 3

    Machine learning and data engineering

    Machine learning in data lakes, data integration and interoperability, data pipelines and streaming.

    Knowledge and data management, information extraction from big data, web data management, pattern recognition, medical imaging, search engines, cloud and IoT services

  4. 4

    Applications

    Machine learning applied to science, health and language.

    Bioinformatics, biomedical informatics, computational biology, healthcare, human activity recognition, computer vision, natural language processing, climate science

Questions

Where are accepted papers published?
All accepted and presented papers are submitted to Procedia Computer Science (Elsevier) for publication and indexing in ScienceDirect, Scopus, Google Scholar, INSPEC and Web of Science, among others.
Can I present online?
ICMLDE strongly encourages presenting in person. Online presentation may be allowed only in exceptional circumstances, such as visa problems, travel restrictions or a medical emergency, at the organisers’ discretion. Request it in writing at least two weeks before the conference.
How long can my paper be?
Between 3 and 10 pages in the conference template. Each page beyond 10 costs ₹1,100 for Indian authors and $15 for international authors.
Where do I find my presentation slot?
The tentative paper presentation schedule (version 01) is on icmlde.org: Find your presentation schedule. Slides and the digital research artwork for your paper were due on 24 November 2026.
How do I get my fee receipt?
Once your payment is confirmed, open Fee receipt, enter your Paper ID and registered email, and print or save the receipt as a PDF.
How do I get a visa invitation letter?
Email info@icmlde.org with your full name as on your passport, passport number and nationality, your Paper ID or proof of registration, and proof of fee payment. Apply for your Indian visa early through the Indian visa portal.
How do I pay the registration fee?
Indian authors pay online through the Paytm link on the official registration form. Foreign authors pay by wire transfer; the account details are under Registration fees.
