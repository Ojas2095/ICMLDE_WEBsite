About ICMLDE 2026

A Premier International Venue for AI & Data Engineering

The 5th International Conference on Machine Learning and Data Engineering (ICMLDE 2026) is an international venue for the presentation of original research findings, as well as the exchange and dissemination of creative, practical development experiences in various engineering domains.

The conference draws researchers and application developers from a wide range of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data engineering subfields. All accepted and presented papers in the primary tracks will be published in open-access Elsevier Procedia Computer Science and indexed in Scopus, Web of Science, and DBLP.

Conference Scope

Conference Sub-Themes & Tracks

Original, unpublished empirical and theoretical contributions are invited across the following primary tracks.

🧠

Machine Learning Foundations & Deep Learning

Machine learning system design, optimization, supervised and unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, statistical learning, transfer learning, and deep neural nets.

ML Optimization Reinforcement Learning Deep Learning Systems Transfer Learning
⚡

Data Engineering & Distributed Computing

Large-scale data pipelines, streaming architectures, zero-ETL data lakes, distributed query engines, database optimization, and cloud telemetry.

Streaming Analytics Data Lakes Distributed Databases Query Optimization
🤖

Generative AI & Agentic Systems

Foundation models, Large Language Models (LLMs), multi-agent reasoning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and reasoning agents for complex automation.

Agentic AI RAG Pipelines LLM Safety Prompt Engineering
🛰️

AI in Remote Sensing, Climate & Healthcare

Multi-modal remote sensing, satellite telemetry, climate forecasting, bio-medical imaging, clinical NLP, and computational genomics.

Remote Sensing Climate AI Biomedical NLP Genomics Models
👁️

Computer Vision, Robotics & IoT

Visual perception, 3D point cloud generation, embodied robotics, edge AI, smart city sensors, and industrial IoT analytics.

Spatial Perception Robotics Navigation Edge Vision IoT Analytics
🛡️

Trustworthy AI, Security & Ethics

Explainable AI (XAI), adversarial robustness, differential privacy, fairness auditing, and regulatory compliance.

Explainable AI Differential Privacy AI Alignment Algorithmic Fairness

Hands-On Sessions

Official Conference Workshops

Specialized technical workshops conducted by industry and academic leaders. Free for registered authors.

Workshop 01

Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing

Resource Person: Dr. L Venkata Subramaniam (IBM Quantum India Leader, IBM Research Lab)

Covers foundational quantum algorithms, Qiskit implementations, and hybrid quantum-classical machine learning for quantum advantage.

Workshop 02

Reinforcement Learning & Reasoning Models

DeepMind & Frontier AI Academic Fellows

Explores modern policy optimization (PPO, GRPO), chain-of-thought verification, and tree-search guidance for frontier mathematical reasoning.

Workshop 03

Agentic AI for Complex Reasoning in Remote Sensing

Geospatial AI & Satellite Telemetry Specialists

Autonomous multi-agent swarms orchestrated to parse multi-spectral satellite imagery, detect land degradation, and predict natural disaster risks.

Workshop 04

Reimagining Software in the Era of Generative AI

Enterprise System Architects & Practitioners

Architecting production LLM applications, deterministic agent tool-use, eval suites, and low-latency lakehouse telemetry.

Author Submission

Paper Submission Guidelines & Live Portal

Submit your regular or innovative research manuscript (up to 10 pages) for Elsevier Procedia publication.

Guidelines for Paper Submission

  • Authors are invited to submit their paper electronically written in English, with a length of up to 10 pages in Elsevier Procedia format.
  • Over the ten (10) page limit, additional pages per paper will be charged.
  • All submitted manuscripts undergo double-blind peer review by at least three independent reviewers.
  • Upon acceptance, at least one author must register to ensure inclusion in the Elsevier Procedia Computer Science proceedings.
