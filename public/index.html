About the conference

The 5th International Conference on Machine Learning and Data Engineering brings together researchers and practitioners working across artificial intelligence, machine learning and data engineering to present original findings and practical experience.

Every accepted and presented paper in the main tracks is published in the open-access Elsevier Procedia Computer Science series and submitted for indexing in Scopus, Web of Science and DBLP. Papers go through double-blind review by at least three reviewers, and best paper awards are given in every track.

The conference runs in hybrid mode: authors can present in person in Dehradun or live online.

Tracks

We invite original, unpublished empirical and theoretical work in six tracks. Choose the closest fit when you submit; the programme committee may move a paper between tracks.

  1. 1

    Machine learning foundations and deep learning

    System design, optimisation, supervised and unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, statistical and transfer learning, deep neural networks.

    ML optimisation, reinforcement learning, deep learning systems, transfer learning

  2. 2

    Data engineering and distributed computing

    Large-scale data pipelines, streaming architectures, data lakes, distributed query engines, database optimisation and cloud telemetry.

    Streaming analytics, data lakes, distributed databases, query optimisation

  3. 3

    Generative AI and agentic systems

    Foundation models, large language models, multi-agent reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation and agents for complex automation.

    Agentic AI, RAG pipelines, LLM safety, prompt engineering

  4. 4

    AI in remote sensing, climate and healthcare

    Multi-modal remote sensing, satellite telemetry, climate forecasting, biomedical imaging, clinical NLP and computational genomics.

    Remote sensing, climate AI, biomedical NLP, genomics

  5. 5

    Computer vision, robotics and IoT

    Visual perception, 3D point clouds, embodied robotics, edge AI, smart-city sensing and industrial IoT analytics.

    Spatial perception, robot navigation, edge vision, IoT analytics

  6. 6

    Trustworthy AI, security and ethics

    Explainable AI, adversarial robustness, differential privacy, fairness auditing and regulatory compliance.

    Explainability, differential privacy, alignment, algorithmic fairness

Propose a special session

Special sessions sit alongside the main tracks and focus on a timely topic. Organisers are expected to bring in at least five or six accepted papers.

Papers in special sessions go through the same double-blind review and are published in Elsevier Procedia Computer Science.

Questions

Where are accepted papers published?
In the open-access Elsevier Procedia Computer Science series, submitted for indexing in Scopus, Web of Science and DBLP. A paper must be registered and presented to be included.
Can I present online?
Yes. The conference is hybrid. You can present in person at UPES Dehradun or live in a virtual session. Choose your mode when you register.
How long can my paper be?
Up to 10 pages in the Elsevier Procedia template. Pages beyond 10 are charged extra.
How do I get my fee receipt?
Once the finance team confirms your payment, open Fee receipt, enter your Paper ID and the email you registered with, and print or save the receipt as a PDF.
I need a visa invitation letter. How do I get one?
After your paper is accepted and you have registered, write to info@icmlde.org and the secretariat will send a signed invitation letter for your visa application.
How can I pay the registration fee?
From India by NEFT, RTGS, IMPS or UPI (icmlde@yesbank). From outside India by SWIFT wire transfer. Account details are in Registration fees.
