Also available: second paper by a regular author (₹11,500 / $250), and the workshop for non-authors (₹7,000 per person; free for registered authors). Pages beyond 10 cost ₹1,100 (Indian) or $15 (international) per page. Fees are non-refundable; a delegate substitution is allowed. All bank charges are paid by the remitter.
