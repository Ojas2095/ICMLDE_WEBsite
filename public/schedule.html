ICMLDE 2026 / Programme

Programme

Two days of keynote talks, a workshop, 30 special sessions and paper presentations, 27–28 November 2026 at the School of Computer Science, UPES, Dehradun.

Presentation schedule. The tentative paper presentation schedule (version 01) was released on 22 November 2026. Find your presentation slot on icmlde.org.

Keynote speakers

Speakers from earlier editions are listed on icmlde.org: distinguished speakers, ICMLDE 1.0–4.0.

  • Dr. Vijayan K. Asari

    University of Dayton, USA

    Ohio Research Scholars Endowed Chair in Wide Area Surveillance and director of the Center of Excellence for Computational Intelligence and Machine Vision (Vision Lab). SPIE Fellow, with more than 600 publications in image processing, computer vision and machine learning.

  • Prof. Björn W. Schuller

    Technical University of Munich, Germany, and Imperial College London, UK

    Chair of Health Informatics at TUM and Professor of Artificial Intelligence at Imperial College London; co-founding CEO and Chief Scientific Officer of audEERING. Fellow of the ACM, IEEE, BCS, ELLIS, ISCA and AAAC.

  • Dr. Sanjay K. Madria

    Missouri University of Science and Technology, USA

    Curators’ Distinguished Professor of Computer Science working on mobile and sensor computing, big data, cloud computing, machine learning and cybersecurity. ACM Distinguished Scientist.

  • Dr. Peter Han Joo Chong

    Auckland University of Technology, New Zealand

    Associate Head of School (Research), School of Engineering, Computer and Mathematical Sciences. Fellow of the IET and listed among the world’s top 2% scientists (Stanford, 2022).

  • Dr. Robertas Damaševičius

    Kaunas University of Technology and Vytautas Magnus University, Lithuania

    Works on explainable AI, computational intelligence, digital health twins, medical imaging and IoT. Editor-in-Chief of Information Technology and Control.

  • Dr. Tanmoy Chakraborty

    IIT Delhi, India

    Professor of Electrical Engineering and associate faculty at the Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence; ACM Distinguished Speaker. Research in NLP, graph neural networks and social computing.

  • Dr. Vachik S. Dave

    Walmart Global Tech, USA

    Principal Data Scientist building large-scale machine learning systems for retail and e-commerce; PhD in Computer Science from Purdue University.

  • Dr. Unal “Zak” Sakoglu

    University of Houston–Clear Lake, USA

    Professor of Computer Engineering applying machine learning to neuroimaging and multidimensional data. IEEE Senior Member.

  • Shri Sudhir Kumar Marwaha

    Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India

    Consultant (Technical) at MeitY and founding Chief Technology Officer of the India Semiconductor Mission, with more than 35 years in electronics and semiconductor policy.

  • Dr. B. K. Murthy

    IIT Bhilai Innovation and Technology Foundation, India

    CEO of IBITF and former Group Coordinator (R&D in IT) at MeitY, where he conceived the Visvesvaraya PhD scheme and led the National Strategy on Blockchain.

Workshop

Free for registered ICMLDE authors, with a certificate for every participant. Non-authors and general participants pay ₹7,000 per person.

  • Workshop 01: Building scalable AI systems, from ML models to production-grade platforms

    Santhosh Vangapelli, Staff Software Engineer and Platform Architect, Coupang, USA

    Santhosh Vangapelli has more than 14 years of experience building large-scale distributed systems, machine learning infrastructure and real-time data platforms, with engineering roles at Coupang, eBay, Indeed and Google. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Massachusetts Amherst and a B.Tech from IIT Kanpur.
