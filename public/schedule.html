ICMLDE 2026 / Programme

Programme

Four keynotes, six tracks of paper sessions, an industry panel and a doctoral colloquium over two days. All times are IST (UTC+5:30).

  1. 08:30–09:30

    Registration, badges and welcome breakfast

    Main foyer. All in-person delegates

  2. 09:30–10:15

    Inaugural ceremony

    Lighting of the lamp, welcome by the general chairs and addresses by invited dignitaries.

    Grand Auditorium. Live-streamed

  3. 10:15–11:15

    Keynote I: Prof. Elena Vance, University of Cambridge

    “Geometric deep learning and the blueprint for next-generation foundational reasoning”

    Grand Auditorium and Zoom Hall 1

  4. 11:15–11:45

    Tea and poster gallery

    Exhibition Lawn

  5. 11:45–13:30

    Parallel paper sessions: Tracks 1 and 2

    Hall A: Track 1, ML and deep learning algorithms (chair: Dr. R. Sharma). Hall B: Track 2, data lakehouses and streaming (chair: Dr. C. Gomez). Zoom Hall 1: online presentations. 15 minutes per paper.

    Halls A and B, Zoom Hall 1

  6. 13:30–14:30

    Lunch

    Convention Dining Hall

  7. 14:30–15:30

    Keynote II: Dr. Arvind Narayanan, Google DeepMind

    “Scaling autonomous agent swarms: safety, verification and coordination”

    Grand Auditorium

  8. 15:30–17:15

    Parallel paper sessions: Tracks 3 and 5

    Hall A: Track 3, generative AI, RAG and LLMs. Hall B: Track 5, computer vision, 3D and robotics.

    Halls A and B

  9. 17:15–18:30

    Industry panel: frontier AI and enterprise scale

    Panellists from Microsoft Research, AWS AI, NVIDIA and startups on compute, data governance and open models.

    Grand Auditorium

  10. 19:30–22:00

    Gala dinner and Himalayan cultural evening

    Uttarakhand music, dinner and informal networking.

    Open-air amphitheatre

  1. 08:45–09:15

    Morning tea

    Main foyer

  2. 09:15–10:15

    Keynote III: Prof. Sunita Krishnan, IISc Bangalore

    “Real-time stream lakehouses: architecting resilient cyber-physical systems”

    Grand Auditorium

  3. 10:15–11:15

    Keynote IV: Prof. Marc Dupont, ETH Zurich

    “Embodied multimodal foundation models for planetary exploration and climate AI”

    Grand Auditorium

  4. 11:15–11:45

    Tea and poster judging

    Poster exhibition area

  5. 11:45–13:30

    Parallel paper sessions: Tracks 4 and 6

    Hall A: Track 4, AI in healthcare, genomics and computational biology. Hall B: Track 6, trustworthy AI, privacy and ethics.

    Halls A and B

  6. 13:30–14:30

    Lunch

    Dining Hall

  7. 14:30–16:00

    Doctoral colloquium and young researcher pitches

    Short talks by PhD students with feedback from senior faculty.

    Seminar Hall 2

  8. 16:00–17:30

    Valedictory and best paper awards

    Best paper awards in all six tracks, certificates, and the announcement of ICMLDE 2027.

    Grand Auditorium. All delegates

Speakers and rooms may change. Check this page on the day.
