Call for papers

We invite original research in machine learning and data engineering. Accepted and presented papers are published in Elsevier Procedia Computer Science, indexed in Scopus.

Submit a paper Check review status

Before you submit

Papers that don’t follow these rules may be returned without review.

  1. 1

    Length and format

    Regular papers: 6 to 10 pages in the Elsevier Procedia template. Short or work-in-progress papers: 4 to 5 pages. Pages beyond 10 are charged extra.

  2. 2

    Anonymise for double-blind review

    Remove author names, emails and affiliations from the PDF. You enter author details in the submission form instead.

  3. 3

    Originality

    The work must be unpublished and not under review elsewhere. The overall similarity index (Turnitin or iThenticate) must not exceed 15%.

  4. 4

    Registration and presentation

    At least one author must register and present the paper, in person or online, for it to appear in the proceedings.

Use the Elsevier Procedia Computer Science template (Word or LaTeX). Find it on the Procedia Computer Science journal page, or write to info@icmlde.org and the secretariat will send it.
