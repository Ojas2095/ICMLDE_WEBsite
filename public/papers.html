Formatting & Integrity

Author Submission Guidelines

Please adhere strictly to Elsevier Procedia guidelines before submitting your manuscript.

1. Manuscript Length & Format

Regular research papers should be between 6 to 10 pages formatted in Elsevier Procedia single/double column layout. Short/Work-in-progress papers: 4 to 5 pages.

2. Double-Blind Review Policy

For the initial review, remove author names, emails, and institutional affiliations from the PDF manuscript to facilitate an unbiased, double-blind peer review.

3. Originality & Plagiarism

Submissions must represent original, unpublished work not under consideration elsewhere. Similarity index (via Turnitin/iThenticate) must not exceed 15% overall.
