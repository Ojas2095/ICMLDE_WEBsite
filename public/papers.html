Call for papers

ICMLDE 2026 invited original, unpublished research papers for presentation and publication in Procedia Computer Science (Elsevier).

Submissions are closed. The submission portal closed on 30 August 2026 (Pacific time). Accepted authors are notified separately.

Submission guidelines

These rules applied to every paper submitted to ICMLDE 2026.

  1. 1

    Length and format

    Papers are written in English, between 3 and 10 pages, in the ICMLDE 2026 template: either a full paper with original results, or a short or innovative paper with a theoretically established idea and practical applications. Each page beyond 10 costs ₹1,100 (Indian authors) or $15 (international authors).

  2. 2

    Double-blind review

    Every manuscript is reviewed double-blind by at least three reviewers. Review is managed centrally by the scientific committee and external reviewers through Microsoft CMT.

  3. 3

    Plagiarism

    Check your paper with a licensed tool such as Turnitin before submitting. Overall similarity must not exceed 10%. Papers whose title or content closely matches a published article are desk-rejected.

  4. 4

    Generative AI

    Read the Elsevier policy on generative AI in scientific writing and the ICMLDE policy before submitting.

  5. 5

    Publication and indexing

    Accepted and presented papers are submitted to Procedia Computer Science (Elsevier), indexed in Web of Science, Scopus, ACM Guide to Computing Literature, INSPEC and EI Compendex, and available on ScienceDirect.

  6. 6

    Best paper awards

    A Best Paper Award and a Best Student Paper Award are presented. Selected top papers, once extended, are considered for WoS- and Scopus-indexed journals. Award details

Themes

Full topic lists are on the home page and in the call for papers.

    Supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement, statistical and transfer learning
  • Deep learning and data engineering
    Deep network optimisation, CNNs, RNNs, generative models

    Data lakes, pipelines, streaming, big data, cloud and IoT services

    Healthcare, bioinformatics, computer vision, NLP, climate science
